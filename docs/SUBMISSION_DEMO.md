# Faro AI Demonstration Guide

## Product access

Faro AI is an authenticated, responsive social-listening workspace for buyer-side service requests on X. It is intentionally **human-led**: the product can surface and organize public posts, but it never sends outreach, posts, replies, messages, or follows.

## Recommended demonstration

| Step | Action | Expected outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open **Search** and choose a starting point such as **Automation**, **Product testing**, or **Dev build**. | Faro prepares a plain-language buyer-demand brief. |
| 2 | Run the search. | One explicit controlled public-source check runs, then Faro screens results for first-party service requests. |
| 3 | Open **Feed**. | Complete qualified posts appear with author identity, full body text, category, signal score, source context, and an **Open X** link. |
| 4 | Select **Review** on a request. | The dedicated review workspace shows the complete post and Faro’s rationale. |
| 5 | Choose **Keep** or **Dismiss**. | The decision is stored internally; no message, reply, follow, post, or external workflow is triggered. |

## Qualification boundary

Faro is designed to retain concrete buyer demand and exclude the common forms of social noise that create false positives.

| Kept | Excluded |
| --- | --- |
| First-person or first-party requests for practical delivered work | Service providers promoting their own offer |
| Requests for AI agents, automation, development, testing, content, video, research, or design support | Jobs, permanent hiring, co-founder searches, and recruiting posts |
| Scoped requests with a provider need or delivery outcome | Networking, community invitations, education, promotions, and generic AI discussion |

> **Human-control boundary.** Faro indexes and ranks public posts for a person to assess. It does not write to X or initiate outreach of any kind.
